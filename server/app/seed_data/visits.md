# Engineer visits

## Visit windows
Each engineer visit window lasts two hours. Visits are booked in these two-hour windows.

## The engineer phones before arriving
The engineer phones you about 30 minutes before arriving, so you know they are on the way.

## Move or cancel a visit
You can move or cancel a visit free of charge up to 24 hours before the window starts. A later change can mean that the next free window is several days away.

## What to do before the visit
1. Clear the space around the appliance.
2. Have the model number ready. It is on a label inside the door.
3. Make sure an adult is at home for the whole window.

## Second visits
If a part must be ordered, the engineer books a second visit for when the part arrives. There is no charge for the second visit.
