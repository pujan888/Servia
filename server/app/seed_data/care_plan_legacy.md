# Northwind Care Plan (legacy plan, sold before 2025)

## What the Care Plan covers (legacy plan)
The legacy Care Plan covers parts and labour for up to 3 appliances. Call-out charges are not covered, and there is no annual service visit.

## Cancel the Care Plan (legacy plan)
The legacy Care Plan can be cancelled within 14 days of sign-up for a full refund. After 14 days it runs until the end of its 12-month term, and cannot be cancelled early.
