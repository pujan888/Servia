# Northwind Care Plan (current plan)

## What the Care Plan covers (current plan)
The current Care Plan covers parts, labour and call-out charges for every appliance bought from Northwind, plus one annual service visit for each appliance.

## When cover starts (current plan)
Cover starts 14 days after sign-up. Faults reported in the first 14 days are not covered.

## Cancel the Care Plan (current plan)
You can cancel the current Care Plan at any time with 30 days' notice. There is no cancellation fee. Payments stop when the notice period ends.

## Make a claim (current plan)
1. Report the fault to us by phone or online.
2. An engineer visits and writes a report.
3. The claims team decides within 5 working days.
4. You receive the decision by email.
